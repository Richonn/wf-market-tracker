# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels
- Focus criticism on ideas, not people
- Accept feedback gracefully

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a private GitHub issue or contacting the maintainer directly. Reports will be reviewed and acted on promptly.
